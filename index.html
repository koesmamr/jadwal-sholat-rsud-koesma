"Allahumma Rabban-naas, adzhibil ba's, isyfi antasy-syaafi, laa syifaa-a illaa syifaa-uk, syifaa-an laa yughaadiru saqamaa."

"Anni massaniyad-dhurru wa anta arhamur-raahimin."
